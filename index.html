How I Work

Design Process

A research-driven, accessibility-first process refined across consulting and agency environments.

🔍
01 · Discover

Discover

Stakeholder interviews, user research and journey mapping to frame the real problem, including what "trustworthy" needs to mean when AI or data drives the product.

🎯
02 · Define

Define

Personas, problem framing and success metrics defined with cross-functional teams, accessibility requirements built in from day one, not bolted on later.

🎨
03 · Design

Design

Wireframes to high-fidelity, human-in-the-loop prototypes, built on scalable Design Systems and validated through usability testing.

🚀
04 · Deliver

Deliver

WCAG 2.2-compliant handoff, close collaboration with engineering, and measuring real-world impact, adoption, trust, accessibility, post-launch.

About Me

A bit about how I got here

I'm a Senior Product & UX/UI Designer with over 7 years of experience driving user-centered, visually impactful digital products across diverse industries. My path has taken me from Design Systems at Jakala to leading UX and UI design across web and mobile platforms at PwC, including end-to-end design on AI-powered products.

I specialise in scalable Design Systems and accessibility standards, and I enjoy the parts of the job most people skip: governance, documentation, and making sure a product still holds together at scale, not just in the first prototype. That same instinct is what I bring to AI-powered products: pushing for interfaces that explain themselves and keep a human in the loop, not just ones that look intelligent.

🌍 Speaks 5 languages, Italian, English, Portuguese, Spanish and French.
