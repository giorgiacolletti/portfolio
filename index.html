How I Work

How I work

The same four steps on most projects, adjusted to the team and the problem.

01

Discover

Stakeholder interviews, user research and journey mapping. When AI or data drives the product, I also work out what people need in order to trust it.

02

Define

Personas, problem statement and success metrics, agreed with the team. Accessibility requirements go in here, not at the end.

03

Design

Wireframes to high-fidelity prototypes on a scalable Design System, tested with users. For AI features, a person stays in the loop.

04

Deliver

WCAG 2.2-compliant handoff, close work with engineering, and checking adoption and accessibility after launch.

About

Giorgia Colletti

I'm a Senior Product & UX/UI Designer with over 7 years of experience driving user-centered, visually impactful digital products across diverse industries. My path has taken me from Design Systems at Jakala to leading UX and UI design across web and mobile platforms at PwC, including end-to-end design on AI-powered products.

I specialise in Design Systems and accessibility. I like the work that tends to get skipped: governance, documentation, making sure a product still holds together at scale and not only in the first prototype. On AI products, I push for interfaces that explain what they do and keep a person in control.

🌍 Speaks 5 languages, Italian, English, Portuguese, Spanish and French.
