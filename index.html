Approach

How I lead design work

The same four steps on most projects. I run them with the team, set the direction, and use AI where it saves time without replacing judgment.

01

Discover

Stakeholder interviews, user research and co-design workshops. AI helps me synthesise findings and test my reading of the brief. When AI drives the product, I work out what people need in order to trust it.

02

Define

Personas, problem statement and success metrics, agreed with product and engineering. Accessibility requirements go in here, not at the end.

03

Design

Prototypes on a scalable Design System, design reviews and mentoring along the way, usability testing with users. For AI features, a person stays in the loop.

04

Deliver

WCAG 2.2-compliant handoff, close work with engineering, and checking adoption and accessibility after launch.

About

Giorgia Colletti

I'm a Senior Product & UX/UI Designer with over 7 years of experience. I built my foundation on the technical side of design: Design Systems at Jakala, WCAG 2.2 accessibility, research and usability testing. At PwC I moved into leading, running UX and UI work across web and mobile platforms and managing and mentoring design teams.

I review work at detail level and run the structure around it: governance, documentation, stakeholder alignment. AI supports that. I use it as a research and production partner, and on AI products I push for interfaces that explain what they do and keep a person in control.
