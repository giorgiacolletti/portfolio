Omnichannel Loyalty App

An e-commerce ecosystem and loyalty app for an Italian retail group with two store brands: one Design System, and one experience online and in store.

RoleProduct & UX/UI Designer
ClientMajor Italian retail group
PlatformE-commerce & mobile app
DeliverablesDesign System, flows, prototypes

The challenge

The client wanted to start a digital transformation and simplify its internal processes on the way to an omnichannel experience. The group runs two store brands, each with its own identity, and needed two new e-commerce sites and a loyalty app that felt like one ecosystem without flattening the difference between the brands.

The design and development teams worked side by side in an Agile way with the project stakeholders, to keep to the time to market.

Three UX principles

Usability

Navigation that is easy to see. Product categories always reachable from the menu, enriched with product lines grouped by consumption style.

Effectiveness

A minimal, clean style with careful spacing and sizing, so the interaction stays simple and focused on products and checkout.

Personalisation

Sections with products and offers based on each customer's purchases, and a repeat-the-shop function for people who want a fast interaction.

What I worked on

  • An omnichannel experience consistent with each brand identity and with what customers expect
  • A creative concept that represents the different identities of the two store brands
  • A Design System with molecules, organisms and scalable components
  • Navigation flows and clickable prototypes
  • Usability tests across different devices, and visual tests to check design quality
  • Integration of the e-commerce into the new loyalty app, unifying the online and offline experience
