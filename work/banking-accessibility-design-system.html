Banking Accessibility & Design System

Accessibility remediation across the web and mobile banking products of a major European banking group, in several countries, working within its Design System and contributing a dedicated iconset.

RoleProduct & UX/UI Designer
ClientMajor European banking group
PlatformWeb & Mobile Banking
FocusWCAG, Design System

Scope

This was part of a wider accessibility programme across international banking platforms. My work covered WCAG compliance roadmaps, inclusive design standards and a share of the accessibility governance, applied to the group's web and mobile banking products.

161Issues logged in one remediation backlog
8Country versions covered

How the work was run

1

Audit

Page-by-page conformance checks against the European standard EN 301 549, which builds on the WCAG criteria. Each result is logged as conformant, non-conformant or not applicable, with notes and the method used, a tool or a manual evaluation.

2

Backlog

Every issue becomes a story grouped under its WCAG criterion, with a severity, the affected country version, the page and a remediation task. In the backlog I worked with, the issues were about colour contrast and the use of colour, and all 161 are marked as done.

3

Remediation

Fixes on design and code together. On the mobile app, issues covered how screen readers read each screen, icon labels and alternative text.

4

Design System

Contributions to the client's Design System, including a dedicated iconset.

What I worked on

  • Design remediation on the mobile banking screens
  • Contributions to the client's Design System, including a dedicated iconset
  • Gap analysis documentation for accessibility compliance
